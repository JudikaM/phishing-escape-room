// 04 · emergency recovery protocol

WHAT TO DO IF YOU FELL FOR IT

Falling for a well-crafted scam can happen to anyone. If you clicked a deceptive link, shared a verification code, or entered payment details, follow these 6 immediate recovery steps:

STEP 01 DISCONNECT

Stop Replying Immediately

Cut all communication with the sender. Ignore follow-up calls, texts, or chat pressure, and never pay a fee to recover lost funds.

STEP 02 OFFICIAL CHANNEL

Contact the Real Service

Reach your bank, carrier, or platform using their official app, your own saved bookmark, or the phone number printed on the back of your card.

STEP 03 CREDENTIAL SECURITY

Change Passwords & Enable 2FA

Reset passwords from a clean device for the affected account and anywhere else you reused that password. Enable app-based two-factor authentication.

STEP 04 FRAUD AUDIT

Audit Recent Account Activity

Review bank statements, active login sessions, linked devices, and email forwarding rules. Freeze payment cards if unknown charges appear.

STEP 05 INCIDENT REPORTING

Report to Service & Authorities

Report the impersonation to the official organization and file an incident report with your national cybercrime or consumer protection agency.

STEP 06 ALERT NETWORK

Warn Contacts & Coworkers

If your email or messaging account was accessed, let friends and colleagues know right away so scammers cannot impersonate you to target them.
